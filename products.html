The materials

Rooted in India. Made for now.

Kokum

कोकम

Pressed from the seed of Garcinia indica, grown along the Konkan coast. High in stearic acid, which is why it sets hard and why it holds on cracked skin instead of vanishing.

Coffee

कॉफ़ी

Shade grown under pepper and cardamom above Coorg and Chikmagalur. We are not claiming anybody’s grandmother scrubbed with coffee. But if you know the smell out of a steel tumbler at seven in the morning, you know what this is.

Rice bran

चावल की भूसी

The light, stable oil that carries the butter and thins it enough to spread. Slower to turn than most plant oils, which is why the balm keeps.
