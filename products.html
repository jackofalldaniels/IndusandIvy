Kokum Butter Balm

Infused with cinnamon. For heels, elbows & knees.

Ingredients
  1. Rice Bran Oil Oryza Sativa (Rice) Bran OilA light oil that sinks in. The base of the balm.
  2. Caprylic/Capric Triglyceride From coconutHelps the balm glide on without a greasy feel.
  3. Kokum Butter Garcinia Indica Seed (Kokum) ButterFrom India's Konkan coast. Softens and conditions dry, rough skin.
  4. Beeswax Cera AlbaKeeps it solid in the jar and in place on skin.
  5. Vitamin E TocopherolA small amount that helps keep the oils fresh.
  6. Cinnamon Leaf Oil Cinnamomum Zeylanicum Leaf OilA warm scent, used at a low level.
How to use

Apply generously to clean, dry heels, elbows and knees. Massage gently until absorbed. Best at night.

Good to know
  • Not vegan: contains beeswax.
  • Not tested on animals. Made in India.
  • For external use only. Avoid contact with eyes.
  • Patch test before first use, and stop if irritation occurs.
  • Diabetic or very sensitive feet? Check with your doctor first.

Why it works

Short list. Every ingredient earns its place.

01

Kokum butter

A firm butter pressed from the kokum seed. It softens at skin temperature and conditions dry, rough skin.

02

No water

An anhydrous balm, so there's nothing to dilute it and no need for preservatives. A little covers a lot.

03

Cinnamon leaf

Chosen over cinnamon bark oil because it's gentler on skin. Used at a low level, for warmth and scent.

The ritual

Warm. Massage. Rest.

Two minutes before bed. Wake up to softer, smoother heels, with steady use over a few weeks.
