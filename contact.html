FAQs

Before you ask.

Where can I buy it?

Nowhere yet. The first run launches on Amazon in the US. Join the list and you'll hear before it goes live.

How much will it cost?

$15 for a 1.76 oz / 50 g jar.

Do you ship outside the US?

Not for the first run. It's a small batch, and we'd rather get one country right first.

Is it vegan?

No. The balm contains beeswax, which keeps it solid in the jar and in place on skin.

Where is it made?

In India, by a manufacturing partner we work with directly. Rahul is based in India and visits the facility.

Is it tested on animals?

No, and it won't be. Our manufacturer doesn't test on animals and neither do we.

Can I use it on my feet if I'm diabetic?

Please check with your doctor first if you're diabetic or have very sensitive feet.
