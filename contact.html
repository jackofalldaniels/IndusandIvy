FAQs

Before you ask.

The balm

What is kokum butter?

A firm butter from the seeds of the kokum fruit, a tree native to India. It softens on warm skin and conditions dry, rough skin. We wrote a full explainer: What is kokum butter?

What does it feel like? Is it greasy?

It's firm in the jar and softens as soon as you warm it between your fingers. It's an oil-based balm, so give it a few minutes to sink in. Kokum butter is often described as feeling lighter than many other butters.

What does it smell like?

Warm and lightly spicy, from cinnamon leaf oil used at a low level. There's no synthetic fragrance.

How do I use it?

Apply generously to clean, dry heels, elbows and knees and massage gently until absorbed. It works best at night. Cotton socks help keep it on your heels and off your sheets.

Can I use it on my lips, face or hands?

We made it for heels, elbows and knees, and that's how we've tested it. We don't recommend it for lips or face. It's fine on dry hands, but it's rich, so use a small amount.

Is it vegan?

No. It contains beeswax, which keeps it solid in the jar and in place on skin.

Can I use it if I'm diabetic or have very sensitive feet?

Please check with your doctor first.

How should I store it?

In a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. On a very hot day it may soften in the jar. That's normal for a butter balm, and it firms up again once it cools.
