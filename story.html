Our Story

The products we grew up with, and couldn't find here.

We're Gaurav, Pratik and Rahul. We grew up in India and now live across Canada, the US and India. We're not chemists. We're three friends who missed the ingredients we were raised on, and decided to make them well.

How it started

A suburb of Mumbai, and a spice box.

We met in high school in a suburb of Mumbai. We came from middle-class homes where kokum, mango and coffee were part of what we ate, and haldi and ubtan were part of how we cared for our skin.

After school, life took us in different directions: engineering, sales, technology, product and marketing. Between us that adds up to more than three decades of work and three master's degrees. What it didn't give us was something of our own.

So we talked about starting a company for months. In December 2025, Rahul and Pratik went to Cosmoprof India to meet manufacturers and makers. We asked family and friends to bring kokum butter and mango butter products back from India so we could use them ourselves and see what actually worked.

Then, in July 2026, Pratik quit his job. That was the day it stopped being a conversation. Fifteen days later, Indus and Ivy was a registered company.

What we believe

India's ingredients deserve to be named for what they are, and where they come from.

How we're learning

We test every sample on our own skin first.

We work closely with our manufacturing partner in India and change the formula when something isn't right. So far, that's meant:

01

Removing the colorant

It only changed how the balm looked. We'd rather give you fewer ingredients that we fully stand behind.

02

Bark oil to leaf oil

We switched from cinnamon bark oil to cinnamon leaf oil after looking closely at how each behaves on skin.

03

Refining the base

Our early samples smelled more oily than we wanted, so we changed the oil in our base.

Our philosophy

Rooted in India. Made for your ritual.

01

Inspired by Ayurveda

We start from ingredients Indian households have relied on for generations, with that tradition as the foundation.

02

Traceable botanicals

Every hero ingredient is named along with where it comes from. Kokum is native to India's Konkan coast.

03

Short ingredient lists

No fillers and no film-formers like dimethicone. Every ingredient is used within established safety limits, and every one is something we can explain.

Why three men are making skin care

Heels crack, elbows get rough, knees dry out, whoever you are. So we make products for the skin everyone has.

What we won't do

You won't see "whitening," "brightening" or "anti-ageing" on anything we make. We'll tell you what an ingredient does and where it comes from. One botanical, one job.

Where it's made

Every jar is made in India by our manufacturing partner, from botanicals grown there.
