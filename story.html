Our Story

Three friends from India, not chemists, are building the products we couldn't find.

We're Gaurav, Pratik, and Rahul. We grew up in India and now live across Canada, the US, and India. We're not chemists by training. We're people who grew up with ingredients like kokum and mango, couldn't find them made well here, and decided to do something about it.

How it started

We met in high school in a suburb of Mumbai. We came from middle-class homes where kokum, mango, and coffee were part of what we ate, and haldi and ubtan were part of how we cared for our skin.

After school, life took us in different directions: engineering, sales, technology, product, and marketing. Between us that adds up to more than three decades of work and three master's degrees. What it didn't give us was something of our own.

So we talked about starting a company for months. In December 2025, Rahul and Pratik went to Cosmoprof India to meet manufacturers and makers. We asked family and friends to bring kokum butter and mango butter products back from India so we could use them ourselves and see what actually worked.

Then, in July 2026, Pratik quit his job. That was the day it stopped being a conversation. Fifteen days later, Indus and Ivy was a registered company.

If you've ever sat on an idea for too long, you know that moment. We won't pretend everything since has been easy, but we're glad we stopped waiting.

How we're learning

Here's what most brands leave out: we're learning skincare formulation as we go.

We work closely with our manufacturing partner in India, test every sample ourselves, and change the formula when something isn't right. So far, that's meant:

Removing the colorant entirely

It only changed how the balm looked. We'd rather give you fewer ingredients that we fully stand behind.

Switching from cinnamon bark oil to cinnamon leaf oil

After looking closely at how each behaves on skin.

Refining the oil in our base

Because our own samples smelled more oily than we wanted.

We're digging into every ingredient: what it does, how much of it matters, and what it works with. That way, what reaches you actually helps. When we learn something that changes a product, we'll tell you. And we would love to incorporate your feedback as we grow.

What we believe

Products should solve a real problem

Not just look good on a shelf.

India's ingredients deserve credit

Kokum, mango, moringa, and more should be named for what they are and where they come from, not blended into "exotic botanicals."

Trust is earned, not claimed

We'll talk about giving back when we have something specific to show you, and not before.

Our philosophy

Inspired by Ayurveda

We start from ingredients Indian households have relied on for generations, chosen with that tradition as the foundation.

Traceable Indian botanicals

Every hero ingredient is named along with where it comes from. For example, kokum is native to India's Konkan coast.

Safe ingredients that work

We use short ingredient lists, with no fillers and no film-formers like dimethicone. Every ingredient is used within established safety limits, and every one is something we can explain.

Where it's made

Every jar is made in India by our manufacturing partner, from botanicals grown there.

Why three men are making skin care

Heels crack, elbows get rough, knees dry out, whoever you are. So we make products for the skin everyone has.

What we won't do

You won't see "whitening," "brightening" or "anti-ageing" on anything we make. We won't promise to change your skin tone or turn back time. We'll tell you what an ingredient does, where it comes from, and that's it: one botanical, one job, a short ingredient list.

We're not chemists

We're three people who learned this one ingredient, one conversation, one sample at a time, and who test everything on our own skin first.

We're starting small, with one product launching soon on Amazon US.
