Where it comes from

Two places on a map.

Konkan coast · Maharashtra & Goa

Kokum

Grows in the strip between the Western Ghats and the sea. The fruit goes into sol kadhi; the seed gets pressed for butter. It has been the answer to split heels there for a very long time, which is less a marketing claim than a description of what people do.

Coorg & Chikmagalur · Karnataka

Coffee

Grown in the shade under pepper vines and cardamom, which is a large part of why Indian arabica tastes the way it does. India has been growing coffee since a pilgrim reportedly carried seven beans back from Yemen in the 1600s.
