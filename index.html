Inside the jar

Two ingredients we grew up with.

Kokum and cinnamon do the main work. The other four are there to help them, and we explain every one.

कोकम

Kokum butter Garcinia indica

A firm butter from the seed of the kokum fruit. It softens on warm skin and conditions dry, rough patches.

Learn more
दालचिनी

Cinnamon leaf oil Cinnamomum zeylanicum

Dalchini, from almost every Indian spice box. A low level of leaf oil gives the balm its warm scent.
