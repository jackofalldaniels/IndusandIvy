Where it comes from

Named for what it is, and where it grows.

We won't blend India's ingredients into "exotic botanicals." Each one is named, with the place it comes from.

कोकम

Kokum (wild mangosteen, Garcinia indica)

Konkan coast, western India

The kokum tree grows in one narrow strip of India's west coast and the hills above it. Its fruit flavours Konkani curries and summer drinks. Its seed is pressed into a firm butter that melts the moment it meets skin. On a coast where the dry season splits heels, that butter has long been the thing people reach for.

दालचिनी

Cinnamon (Cinnamomum zeylanicum)

Malabar coast, southwestern India

Dalchini sits in almost every Indian spice box. We use oil from the cinnamon leaf, which is gentler on skin than oil from the bark, and we keep it at a low level. It's there for the warm scent when you open the jar.
