Where it comes from

Named for what it is, and where it grows.

We won't blend India's ingredients into "exotic botanicals." Each one is named, with the place it comes from.

कोकम

Kokum Garcinia indica

Konkan coast, western India

The kokum tree grows in one narrow strip of India's west coast. Its seed is pressed into a firm butter that melts the moment it meets skin. On a coast where the dry season splits heels, it has long been the thing people reach for.

दालचिनी

Cinnamon Cinnamomum zeylanicum

Malabar coast, southwestern India

Dalchini sits in almost every Indian spice box. We use oil from the cinnamon leaf, which is gentler on skin than oil from the bark, at a low level. It's there for the warm scent when you open the jar.

The ritual

Two minutes, every night.

  1. Warm

    Scoop a small amount and warm it between your fingertips until it softens.

  2. Massage

    Work it into clean, dry heels, elbows and knees with slow, firm strokes.

  3. Rest

    Let it sink in overnight. With steady use, skin feels softer and smoother within a few weeks.
