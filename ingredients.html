Ingredients

Six ingredients. Every one explained.

Here's everything in our Kokum Butter Balm, in label order, heaviest first, with what each ingredient does and why we chose it.

01

Rice Bran Oil

Oryza Sativa (Rice) Bran Oil

What it is

A light oil pressed from the outer layer of the rice grain.

Why it's here

It's the base of the balm. It sinks in easily and carries the butter across the skin.

02

Caprylic/Capric Triglyceride

Derived from coconut

What it is

A light, silky oil made from coconut.

Why it's here

It helps the balm glide on without a heavy, greasy feel.

03

Kokum Butter

Garcinia Indica Seed (Kokum) Butter

What it is

A firm butter from the seeds of the kokum fruit. Kokum grows in India, and many Indian families know it first from the kitchen, in curries and cooling summer drinks.

Why it's here

It's solid at room temperature and softens on contact with warm skin. It softens and conditions dry, rough skin, which is why it's the hero of the balm.

Good to know

Kokum butter isn't cold-pressed. Our supplier uses a process suited to extracting the solid fat from kokum seeds, which gives it its firm texture.

04

Beeswax

Cera Alba

What it is

Natural wax made by honey bees.

Why it's here

It keeps the balm solid in the jar and helps it stay in place on skin. It's also why the balm isn't vegan.

05

Vitamin E

Tocopherol / Mixed Tocopherols

What it is

An antioxidant found naturally in plant oils.

Why it's here

A small amount helps keep the oils in the balm fresh.

06

Cinnamon Leaf Oil

Cinnamomum Zeylanicum Leaf Oil

What it is

An essential oil from the leaf of the cinnamon tree. Dalchini, the bark, sits in almost every Indian spice box.

Why it's here

It gives the balm its warm scent. We use it at a low level.

Good to know

Our early samples used cinnamon bark oil. We switched to leaf oil after looking closely at how each behaves on skin.

What we leave out

If it doesn't have a job, it doesn't go in.

Water

So there's nothing to dilute the butter, and no need for preservatives.

Preservatives

Not needed in a water-free balm.

Synthetic fragrance

The scent comes from cinnamon leaf oil.

Colorants

We took the colorant out. It only changed how the balm looked.

Film-formers

No dimethicone or silicones.

What we won't say

You won't see "whitening," "brightening" or "anti-ageing" on anything we make. We won't promise to change your skin tone or turn back time. We'll tell you what an ingredient does, and that's it.

What's next

Moringa seed oil is next: 100% virgin and cold-pressed. As we add products, we'll add every new ingredient to this page.
